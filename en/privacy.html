Privacy policy

Effective from 20 November 2026

This policy describes how personal data is handled by the SkiLog app for Android, the skilog.cz website and the beta test sign-up. In short: your runs and your location stay on your phone. SkiLog has no server of its own and no user accounts.

The SkiLog app

Location and ski data

While recording, the app processes your GPS location, speed, altitude and time, also when the phone is locked or the app runs in the background. It also stores your profile (name or nickname, username, chosen sport, avatar colour) and your settings.

  • Everything is stored only in a database on your phone. It is not sent to us or anyone else.
  • You can delete single days and profiles in the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes everything.
  • A GPX file is created by you when you export a day, and you decide who you send it to.

Android backup

If Google backup is turned on on your phone, Android may back up the app's data, including your ski history, to your Google account. The app allows this only when the backup is encrypted with your phone's screen lock. This follows your phone settings and Google's terms; we have no access to the backup.

Maps and resorts

To show a map, the app downloads map tiles from the OpenTopoMap and OpenSnowMap servers. Like any website, these servers see your phone's IP address and the map area you are viewing. When the map shows your current position, your approximate location can be inferred from it. When you open a resort's details, the app asks the Overpass API (OpenStreetMap) for the names of its runs and lifts. We do not send your recorded days to these services. The resort catalogue is part of the app.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the app crashes, it sends a report to Firebase Crashlytics, a service of Google. The report contains a technical description of the error, the app version, the phone model, the Android version and a random installation ID. It does not contain your location, ski data or profile name.

  • Purpose: finding and fixing bugs. Legal basis: legitimate interest in a reliable app.
  • You can turn crash reports off at any time in the app's Settings.
  • Google keeps the reports for 90 days. It may process them outside the EU, based on standard contractual clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

The skilog.cz website

The website uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., which records IP addresses of visitors for security and operation of the site (GitHub's privacy statement).

If you write to us at info@skilog.cz, we use your address and your message only to reply, and keep them only as long as needed to deal with it.

Beta test

You sign up for the beta test with a form on Tally (Tally BV, Belgium). The form opens only after you click “Join the beta”; until then the website sends nothing to Tally. We process the following data from it:

  • Data: e-mail, phone model and Android version, and your note if you add one.
  • Purpose: inviting you to the beta, sending you the link to install the test version and arranging feedback.
  • Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by e-mail to info@skilog.cz.
  • We distribute test versions through Firebase App Distribution or Google Play. We give them your e-mail so they can send you the invitation and download link.
  • Retention: until the end of the beta test, at most 6 months after the app is released on Google Play. Then we delete the sign-ups.
  • Feedback and GPX files you choose to send us are used only to improve the app and are deleted together with your sign-up. A GPX file contains the route of the day, so only send the ones you want to share.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict processing, to data portability, to object and to withdraw consent. Just write to info@skilog.cz; we reply within one month. Data in the app is out of our reach; you manage it directly on your phone.

If you believe we handle your data in breach of the law, you can lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz) or the data protection authority in your country.

Changes to this policy

If the way we process data changes, we will update this policy and its effective date. Significant changes, for example if data started leaving the phone because of cloud sync, will be announced in advance, also in the app.
